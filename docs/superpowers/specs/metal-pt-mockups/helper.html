Metal-PT Helper will briefly pause charging and watch the battery current. Keep the charger connected, battery below 95 %. About 2 minutes.

✓ Charger connected, battery 72 %
✓ AlDente not running
✓ Found charge key CHTE
◐ Pausing charging… battery current 1 820 → 340 → 0 mA
○ Restoring charging and confirming current rises
○ Saving result for this Mac (Mac16,5, macOS 26.6)
If any step fails, the original bytes are written back, charging is restored, and the feature stays off with the reason shown.

Fans get the same kind of check: take one fan to a target speed, confirm the RPM really changes, hand it back, confirm macOS takes over. Macs without fans (Air) or battery (mini/Studio) just don't show those sections.
