The in-game overlay

Click-through, never takes focus, sits above fullscreen games. Toggle with ⌃⇧F7. While recording it shows ● REC and the time. Which style should be the default? (The other can be a setting.)

A

Compact strip

One line in a corner. Least in the way; good for playing.

B

Detailed panel

A small block with labelled rows and a frame-time sparkline. Good for testing settings.

C

Compact, expands while recording

Strip while playing; switches to the panel automatically during a recording, back after.
